Same control set, three treatments, evaluated side by side. Every control is shown in all five states — rest / hover / active / focus-visible / disabled — using forced state classes (.s-hover, .s-active, .s-focus, .s-disabled) so the full matrix is visible at once. Each treatment also has a LIVE strip at the bottom of every section: hover, click, and Tab through those to confirm the forced states match real behavior. Motion is deceleration-only, ≤ 220 ms, no loops, no elastic easing, no colored glow — focus is a solid offset ring. All control borders use --control-edge on --card/--card-2 surfaces (≈ 3.5:1).

System rules applied to all three treatments

Motion
  • Single easing curve, cubic-bezier(0.20,0.70,0.20,1) — deceleration only, no overshoot or elastic return.
  • 110 ms for press feedback, 150 ms for color/border, 220 ms ceiling. Nothing animates on load, nothing loops.
  • Only state-carrying properties move: background, border, color, 1 px press offset, edge-marker width.
  • prefers-reduced-motion collapses all durations to 1 ms.
Contrast & focus
  • Every control border is --control-edge on --card / --card-2 / --bg → ≈ 3.3–3.9:1, above the 3:1 non-text minimum. --line and --line-2 are reserved for structural dividers only, never control edges.
  • Focus is a solid --accent-c outline with a 2–3 px offset — a ring, not a glow. No blurred or colored shadows anywhere in the file.
  • Disabled controls drop to 42 % opacity, the one intentional sub-3:1 case (WCAG exempts inactive controls).
  • Status is never color-only: every badge and cell carries a text label beside its dot.
8-hour / gloved-hand recommendation
  • Ship C at the machine. 48 px targets survive gloves, recessed wells keep operators from stabbing an input expecting an action, and the edge marker reads in peripheral vision while their eyes are on the spindle.
  • Ship B for the wall board and supervisor view. Flat, uppercase, 2 px outlines hold up at 1.5 m and in glare; no depth means nothing to misread as a live state.
  • Keep A for the dense queue screen only — the press affordance is the best of the three, but 40 px targets are the first thing to fail with gloves on.
  • All three share one token set and one motion spec, so mixing them across screens stays coherent.
